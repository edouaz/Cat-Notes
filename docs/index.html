From the first scribble
to the next good idea.

A few useful tools, close at hand. Bring a document, start a fresh page, or pick up where you left off.

01 / WRITE

A pen that feels like yours.

Pen, pencil, highlighter, eraser and lasso. Choose a color and width, and see a real stroke preview before you start.

02 / COLLECT

Keep the little things together.

Folders, favorites and open notebook tabs. Add PDFs, images, text and sticky notes to your pages.

03 / KEEP

Your notes, on your device.

Start without an account. Export a PDF to share, or a native .catnote backup to keep editing. Your notebooks have a way out.

Small steps, out in the open

A prototype.
With room to grow.

OpenNotes is far from a final release. We are sharing it early so you can explore the app, understand the code and help decide what comes next.

Read the build 9 release notes

A few honest notes.

The IPA needs signing before installation. Physical Apple Pencil performance, older iPads and accessibility still need real-world validation.

Cloud sync is experimental. Export backups, and keep another copy of important notes.
