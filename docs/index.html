01 / A notebook, at your pace

From a passing thought
to a page worth keeping.

WRITE

Make your mark.

Pen, pencil, highlighter and a custom instrument bar. Find your color, set your width and see how your next stroke will look.

COLLECT

Keep things together.

Organize notebooks in folders, mark favorites and move between open tabs. Bring PDFs, images and text onto the page.

TAKE WITH YOU

Your notes have a way out.

Export a PDF to share, or a native .catnote backup to keep editing. Local storage works without an account.

02 / Early, and out in the open

A prototype.
With room to grow.

Cat Notes is far from a final release. Sharing it now means you can explore what exists, see how it works and help shape what comes next.

Read the build 8 release notes ↗

A few honest notes.

The IPA needs signing before installation. Physical Apple Pencil performance, older iPads and accessibility still need real-world validation.

Cloud sync is experimental. Export backups, and keep another copy of important notes.

03 / Make it your own

System frameworks. A checked-in Xcode project. No package installation or backend required for local use.
